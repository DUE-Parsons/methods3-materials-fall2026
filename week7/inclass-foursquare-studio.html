In-class Exercises

Foursquare Studio

  1. Download today's data if you haven't already.
  2. Create a Foursquare Studio account.
  3. Upload the Inside Airbnb CSV. Under Datasets, click + New Dataset.
  4. Create a map with the dataset.
  5. Style the points with a simple style.
  6. Style the points' radii and fills by fields such as price.
  7. Try other styles such as heatmaps.
  8. Try adding filters.
  9. Try side-by-side.
  10. Publish (Share in the left sidebar). Copy the link, which will be public. Confirm it works in your browser.
