In-class Exercises

Apply cartographic concepts while looking at a map

For this exercise, you will be part of a group of 2 or 3.

  1. Open the NYC Subway Map.
  2. Look at the map and discuss the cartography. Consider the carographic principles we discussed (scale, hierarchy, color, symbology). What works about the map? What doesn't work about the map?
