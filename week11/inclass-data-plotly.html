Data Plotly

  1. Download today's data.
  2. Open QGIS and open the shapefile in data-plotly.
  3. Install the Data Plotly plugin.
  4. Open Data Plotly: Plugins > Data Plotly > Data Plotly plugin.
  5. Make a scatterplot of ghlth_crudeprev (poor health) versus pct_eli_all (extremely low income).
  6. Explore outliers by clicking on the dots in the scatterplot and finding them in your map.
