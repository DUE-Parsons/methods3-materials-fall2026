In-class Exercises

Datawrapper

  1. Go to Datawrapper and create a login if you don't have one.
  2. Create a new map. (Create New > Map in the top menu.)
  3. Choose Choropleth map.
  4. Under "What type of map do you want to create?" search for counties and select USA > Counties (2023). Click Proceed.
  5. Upload data, select the CSV in the renters_owners_census_acs_2023_5yr folder that was in our zip file.
  6. In the table, scroll all the way to the right and click the options button (three dots) next to either p_owners or p_renters, then under Use column for, select Values. These columns are the percent of households that are owner-occupied or renter-occupied, respectively.
  7. Click Proceed and choose options under Visualize as you see fit.
  8. Move along to Publish & Embed, Publish Now, and get the public link to your visualization.
